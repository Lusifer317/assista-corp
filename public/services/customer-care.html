Operating boundaries

Clear responsibilities protect the work.

Support is most effective when the team knows what it can prepare or execute, what needs review, and what must stay with the client. These boundaries should be written into the engagement scope.

What support can cover

Routine enquiries, ticket classification, approved responses, information gathering, documented troubleshooting, and escalation coordination.

What stays under your control

Policy changes, refunds or credits outside pre-approved rules, legal or safety-sensitive complaints, account security decisions, and any customer commitment requiring an exception or business judgment.

Operational visibility

Choose measures that reflect the work—not vanity metrics.

The measures below are examples to agree at kickoff. Baselines, definitions, targets, data sources, and reporting cadence should be set for each engagement.

First responseTime from request arrival to the first meaningful response, segmented by channel and service window.
Resolution and handoffTime to resolution, repeat contacts, backlog age, and escalation volume.
Quality and customer feedbackQuality-review results, policy adherence, and customer satisfaction where you collect it.

No response-time, savings, placement, or productivity target is implied by this page. Any service level is agreed only after workload and coverage are understood.

Before you enquire

Is this support model right for your team?

The strongest starting point is a repeatable process with a clear owner, an agreed definition of done, and a review path for exceptions.

Often a good fit when

A good fit when customer requests follow repeatable patterns and there is an approved knowledge base, policy set, and escalation owner.

What to bring to the first conversation

Bring channel volumes, operating hours, common request types, current helpdesk workflow, brand guidance, and examples of issues that must be escalated.

Frequently asked questions

Questions worth answering upfront.

Expand a question to review the practical considerations for this service.

Can support operate in our existing helpdesk?

That is the preferred model where access and platform fit allow it. The specific system, roles, and data access are validated during scoping.

Can you provide 24/7 support?

Coverage is not assumed. We will discuss required hours, weekends, languages, and volume, then confirm whether the requested schedule can be staffed.

Who decides refunds and exceptions?

Your business retains policy ownership and approval. Support can collect information and route requests, and may perform only actions explicitly authorized in your written rules.

How is customer experience quality reviewed?

Agree a quality rubric covering accuracy, tone, policy adherence, documentation, resolution, and escalation. Reporting cadence and sample sizes are set for the engagement.
