Operating boundaries

Clear responsibilities protect the work.

Support is most effective when the team knows what it can prepare or execute, what needs review, and what must stay with the client. These boundaries should be written into the engagement scope.

What support can cover

Administrative finance processing, reconciliation preparation, invoice tracking, document organization, and reporting support within client-approved systems and procedures.

What remains with your authorized finance owners

Payment release, bank transfers, approval of journals, sign-off of accounts, tax positions, statutory filings, audit opinions, and regulated financial decisions. Support is not audit, tax, or accounting advice.

Operational visibility

Choose measures that reflect the work—not vanity metrics.

The measures below are examples to agree at kickoff. Baselines, definitions, targets, data sources, and reporting cadence should be set for each engagement.

Reconciliation statusAccounts or statements reconciled, items pending, and age of unmatched transactions.
Exception handlingOpen variances, documentation gaps, owner assigned, and time to resolution.
Close readinessCompletion of agreed schedules, missing records, and outstanding approvals before close.

No response-time, savings, placement, or productivity target is implied by this page. Any service level is agreed only after workload and coverage are understood.

Before you enquire

Is this support model right for your team?

The strongest starting point is a repeatable process with a clear owner, an agreed definition of done, and a review path for exceptions.

Often a good fit when

A good fit when the team has repeatable finance administration and reconciliation work that can be prepared and reviewed under clear controls.

What to bring to the first conversation

Bring a sample workflow, systems in use, transaction volumes, close dates, document types, and your current approval matrix. Do not send live bank credentials in an inquiry.

Frequently asked questions

Questions worth answering upfront.

Expand a question to review the practical considerations for this service.

Can Assista release payments or move funds?

No. The service scope is preparation, tracking, and reconciliation support. Payment release and fund movement remain with your authorized signatories.

Do you replace our accountant or auditor?

No. Administrative support can help organize records and prepare schedules, but it does not replace a qualified accountant, tax adviser, auditor, or controller.

Which accounting tools do you support?

Tool fit should be confirmed during discovery. We will validate the platform, permission model, data-export options, and workflow before confirming scope.

How are discrepancies handled?

Exceptions should be documented with source references, amount or record identifiers, issue category, and assigned client reviewer. The client decides the accounting treatment.
