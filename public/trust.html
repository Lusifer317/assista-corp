Trust & Assurance

Trust should be verifiable—not just a badge.

Business support can involve access to sensitive workflows, systems and records. We believe the right starting point is clear scope, controlled access, documented approvals and evidence that can be reviewed.

Credential transparency

We publish credentials only when they can be verified.

This page is a proposed assurance roadmap and an explanation of the evidence we believe clients should be able to review. It does not represent Assista Corp as currently certified to an ISO standard, having a SOC 2 report, or holding a third-party security accreditation. A credential will be described as achieved only after its holder, scope, issuer, validity and supporting evidence have been checked.

Engagement safeguards

Define the controls before work begins.

The safeguards below are the operating topics to document with each client. The exact control set depends on the systems, data, work location and contractual scope.

01

Access by role

Agree named user accounts, least-privilege access, approved devices and systems, MFA expectations, access reviews and prompt removal when a role ends.

02

Written scope and instructions

Document the tasks, information handled, systems used, service hours, quality checks, data-retention expectations and escalation contacts.

03

Approval boundaries

Separate preparation from authorization. Payment release, financial sign-off, hiring decisions, policy exceptions and sensitive customer commitments remain with client-authorized owners.

04

Quality and exception review

Agree review samples, evidence required, defect and exception categories, owners, corrective actions and a reporting cadence.

05

Incident and escalation handling

Define how suspected data exposure, account misuse, service disruption, urgent cases and material process errors are reported and escalated.

06

Exit and access removal

Agree how client data, work product, credentials, devices and outstanding tasks are returned, transferred or securely removed at the end of an engagement.

These are scoping principles, not a claim that every control has already been independently audited or certified. The final controls must be documented and evidenced for the specific engagement.

Company assurance roadmap

Standards worth building toward.

The following are proposed targets for a business-support provider. They are not badges or certifications currently being claimed on this website.

Information security managementProposed target

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Build an information security management system (ISMS) covering risk assessment, information assets, access, supplier management, incident response, business continuity interfaces, internal audit and continual improvement.

  • Define the ISMS scope and interested parties
  • Complete risk assessment and treatment plan
  • Implement and evidence selected controls
  • Complete internal audit and management review
  • Use an accredited certification body for certification
Read the ISO/IEC 27001 standard overview ↗
Quality managementProposed target

ISO 9001:2026

Establish a quality management system (QMS) for consistent service delivery, customer requirements, process ownership, performance evaluation, corrective action and continual improvement. ISO published the 2026 edition in September 2026.

  • Map service delivery processes and owners
  • Define quality objectives and customer feedback
  • Track nonconformities and corrective actions
  • Review supplier and service performance
  • Prepare for independent certification when ready
Read the ISO 9001:2026 overview ↗
US enterprise assuranceEvaluate buyer demand

SOC 2 examination

SOC 2 is an independent CPA examination and report against applicable AICPA Trust Services Criteria—not an ISO-style certification. Evaluate the required scope and report type with enterprise prospects before committing to an examination.

  • Map security, availability and confidentiality requirements
  • Assess control design and evidence readiness
  • Determine whether Type I or Type II fits buyer needs
  • Engage a properly licensed independent CPA firm
  • Share any resulting report under its permitted-use terms
Read the AICPA SOC resources ↗
UK supplier assuranceLater-market option

Cyber Essentials

Consider the UK scheme if UK procurement requirements make it commercially useful. It focuses on five technical control areas: firewalls, secure configuration, security updates, user access control and malware protection.

  • Confirm the UK buyer and scope requirements
  • Assess endpoints, cloud services and user access
  • Remediate gaps before assessment
  • Use the official scheme route and keep certification current
Read the NCSC Cyber Essentials overview ↗

Certification is organization- and scope-specific. Implementing a standard, being assessed, and holding a valid certificate are different states. Only the final verified state should be presented as “certified”.

Healthcare workflows

Privacy obligations must be scoped—not assumed.

There is no HHS-recognized “HIPAA certification” for a business associate. Where an Assista engagement creates, receives, maintains or transmits protected health information on behalf of a covered entity or business associate, the parties must assess their roles, execute the required business associate agreement where applicable, and define safeguards, workforce training, access, incident reporting and subcontractor requirements.

We will not describe a service as “HIPAA certified” or make blanket compliance promises. Any healthcare engagement must be assessed against the actual data, service, contracts and operating controls.

Illustrative workflows

Examples of the work we can help structure.

These examples explain how a service could be scoped. They are not client testimonials, case studies or claims about completed customer outcomes.

Illustrative scenario · E-commerce

Customer support queue

Define order-status enquiries, returns intake, response templates, priority categories and escalation to the client's authorized team.

Measures to agree
  • First-response time by channel
  • Backlog age and repeat contacts
  • Escalation and quality-review results
Illustrative scenario · Finance

Reconciliation preparation

Organize approved source statements, match transactions using agreed rules, maintain an exception register and route unresolved items to the client reviewer.

Measures to agree
  • Schedule completeness
  • Unmatched-item count and age
  • Reviewer sign-off and open exceptions
Illustrative scenario · Executive office

Leadership coordination

Coordinate calendars, prepare meeting material, maintain follow-up actions and return sensitive decisions or commitments to the executive for approval.

Measures to agree
  • Meeting-preparation completeness
  • Overdue action items
  • Scheduling rework and conflicts

No customer identity, testimonial, conversion rate, savings figure or operational result is implied by these examples.
